# Accessible content

## Plain language
Write short sentences with common words, and explain abbreviations on first use. Plain language helps readers with cognitive disabilities, non-native readers and everyone reading on a phone.

## Images and colour
Give every informative image alternative text that conveys its purpose, and mark decorative images as decorative. Never use colour alone to carry meaning, and keep text contrast at least 4.5 to 1.

## Video and audio
Provide captions for all video and a transcript for audio. Describe important visual information in the narration or in the text.

## Structure
Use real headings, lists and tables rather than visual formatting, so assistive technology can navigate the content.
