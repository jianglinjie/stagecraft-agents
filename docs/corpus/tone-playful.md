# Playful tone

## Voice rules
A playful voice is warm, curious and light, never silly about the reader's problem. Use short sentences, second person and everyday verbs. Humour comes from surprising comparisons, not from jokes at anyone's expense.

## Headlines
Playful headlines promise a benefit with a twist: a question, a small contradiction or a vivid verb. Keep them under ten words and make sure they still say what the piece is about.

## What to avoid
Avoid sarcasm, memes that date quickly and slang that excludes non-native readers. Use at most one exclamation mark per section and no emoji in headings.

## Keeping it accurate
Playful does not mean vague. Every light sentence still needs to be true, and numbers, limits and steps are written plainly.
