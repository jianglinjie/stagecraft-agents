# Outline patterns

## Choosing a pattern
Pick the outline pattern from the reader's question. "How do I" suggests a step-by-step tutorial, "which should I choose" a comparison, "why does this happen" a problem and solution piece, and a list of recurring questions an FAQ.

## Step-by-step tutorial
A tutorial states the goal and prerequisites, then numbers the steps, one action per step, and ends by verifying the result. Each step shows the expected outcome.

## Comparison
A comparison names the criteria before the options, evaluates every option against the same criteria, and ends with a recommendation per situation rather than a single winner.

## Section counts
Three to five sections suit most articles. Fewer than three usually means the piece is a note; more than seven usually means it should be a series.
