# Planning a multi-part series

## Each part stands alone
A reader may arrive at any part first. Every part needs its own introduction and a complete takeaway, with a short recap of earlier parts instead of assuming they were read.

## Shared research, separate drafts
Research the topic once and derive every part from the same brief and outline, then write a separate draft for each part. This keeps facts consistent without making parts repeat each other.

## Naming and navigation
Use a consistent title pattern that includes the part number, and link each part to the previous and next ones. Announce the full list of parts in the first one.

## Cadence
Publish on a predictable schedule and finish writing at least two parts before publishing the first, so a delay does not stall the series.
