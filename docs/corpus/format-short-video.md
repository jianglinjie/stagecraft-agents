# Short video script

## Hook in the first seconds
The first three seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching. Open on the problem or the result, not on a logo or an introduction.

## Length and pacing
Keep short videos between 60 and 90 seconds, with one message. Change the shot or the on-screen text every few seconds and cut every sentence that does not serve the message.

## Captions and silent viewing
Most short videos are watched without sound. Burn in captions, and make sure the on-screen text alone tells the story.

## Call to action
End with one call to action that matches the message, shown on screen and spoken. Two calls to action compete and both lose.
