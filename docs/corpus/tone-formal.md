# Formal tone

## Voice rules
A formal voice is precise, neutral and impersonal. Prefer the third person, avoid contractions and colloquialisms, and choose the exact term over the familiar one.

## Claims and evidence
Every claim is qualified to what can be supported. Attribute figures to their source, give dates for anything that changes, and distinguish measured results from expectations.

## Structure
Formal documents use descriptive headings, numbered lists for sequences and tables for comparisons. Each section opens with a sentence stating its purpose.

## What to avoid
Avoid rhetorical questions, humour and intensifiers such as "very" or "extremely". Passive voice is acceptable when the actor is irrelevant, but not to hide who is responsible.
